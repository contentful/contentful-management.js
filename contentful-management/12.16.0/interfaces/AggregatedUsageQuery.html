Optionalorder

order?: string

Comma-separated columns in sys.dimensions.<name>.sys.<suffix> form (matching the group and filter grammar), optionally prefixed with - for descending, e.g. "total_usage" or "-total_usage" (descending). The synthetic aggregate total_usage is a bare-token passthrough ("total_usage", "-total_usage"). For api_call_* metrics, total_usage sorts by the per-group sum across the requested date range.
